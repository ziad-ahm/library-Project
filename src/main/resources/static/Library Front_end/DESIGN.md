## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, curated salon aesthetic tailored for high-end literary experiences. It fuses the tactile warmth of a classic private library with the disciplined restraint of modern minimalist interface design.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Cultivated & Discerning:** Evoking the prestige of rare book dealers, high-end publishing houses, and literary institutions.
- **Warm & Hospitable:** Grounded in comforting ivory canvas tones and sunbaked terracotta hues that prevent the interface from feeling cold or purely transactional.
- **Scholarly Clarity:** Elevated typography establishes immediate authority while maintaining flawless readability across long-form browsing and reading contexts.

### Design Movement & Influence
The design style integrates **Modern Warm Minimalism** with **Editorial Craft**. It avoids harsh industrial lines and heavy skeuomorphism in favor of:
- Generous, intentional negative space that allows book jackets and typography to breathe.
- Hairline structural framing and quiet tonal shifts rather than aggressive drop shadows.
- Distinct typographic pairing: an authoritative editorial serif for titles and discovery moments, backed by a balanced, technical grotesque sans-serif for high-density catalog interactions and transactional UI.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in artisanal pigments: sun-baked terracotta, antique botanical green, warm gold leaf, and deep ink slate, suspended against a layered foundation of warm ivory and cream.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C05A3E`):** A warm, rich terracotta accenting critical interaction points, primary calls to action, active navigation tabs, and badge highlights.
- **Secondary (`#2B4C3F`):** A deep, restrained forest green used for secondary accents, literary tags, verified status badges, and specialized editorial collection highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B52`):** A muted warm amber utilized for star ratings, curated highlights, award seals, and subtle warm notification pips.
- **Neutral (`#2C2A29`):** A deep charcoal ink used for high-contrast typography and prominent structural elements, replacing generic harsh black.

### Surface Tones & Canvas Application
- **Base Canvas (`#FBF9F5`):** Soft warm ivory setting the overall atmospheric tone without degrading readability.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white applied to interior cards, search inputs, modal dialogs, and interactive popovers to provide luminous contrast against the ivory field.
- **Surface Muted (`#F2EFEB`):** Subtle tinted beige for table headers, secondary button resting states, pill backgrounds, and soft divider backings.
- **Border Subtle (`#E6E0D8`):** Low-contrast architectural hairpins separating content zones and outlining surface containers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Libre Caslon Text** for prominent literary display moments with **Manrope** for legible, modern interface navigation and data presentation.

### Usage Directives
- **Headlines (`Libre Caslon Text`):** Used strictly for editorial introductions, book titles, author showcases, section headers, and curated collection themes. It brings craftsmanship, intellectual gravitas, and vintage warmth.
- **Interface & Long-Form Body (`Manrope`):** Used for navigation bars, book synopses, shopping carts, filters, specs, and form inputs. Manrope's neutral proportions balance Caslon's expressive curves.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered in `Manrope` 600 weight with subtle letter-spacing (`0.05em` - `0.06em`) in small capitals where appropriate to evoke fine editorial binding tags and spine categorizations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model balances strict geometric alignment with generous, editorial white space.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container of `1360px`, framed by `margin: 3rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Ideal for book catalog arrangements spanning 3, 4, or 6 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Book grid items reflow to 2 or 4 columns.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column responsive grid with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Catalog items adopt a 2-column card layout or single-column detailed list.

### Spacing Philosophy
Internal component padding enforces rhythm:
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled visual units such as book title to author name, badge internal padding, and icon-to-label gaps.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for standard container padding, input fields, and compact list gaps.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for card body padding, filter drawer separation, and modular section headers.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) between distinct editorial segments and multi-column catalog shelves.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, using a combination of **tonal surface layering** and **warm ambient illumination** to create tactile depth reminiscent of physical paper stock and display vitrines.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Floor (`#FBF9F5`):** The default window canvas representing unbleached archival paper.
2. **Elevated Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Book product cards, dropdown menus, flyouts, and reader panels. Resting on the base floor with an ultra-soft border (`1px solid #E6E0D8`).
3. **Floating Overlays:** Modals, shopping bags, and quick-view trays.

### Shadow Recipe & Character
When elevated elements detach from the canvas, they project diffused, warm-tinted shadows rather than cold grays:
- **Card Rest:** Flat with hairline border `1px solid #E6E0D8`.
- **Card Hover:** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(44, 42, 41, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(44, 42, 41, 0.04)`. Border shifts subtly toward terracotta tint `rgba(192, 90, 62, 0.25)`.
- **Floating Modals / Popovers:** `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(44, 42, 41, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 42, 41, 0.05)`.
- **Book Cover Display:** Individual cover art uses a distinct spine shadow: `inset 4px 0 6px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.18), 3px 6px 16px -2px rgba(44, 42, 41, 0.18)` to reproduce the tactile physical volume of a bound hardcover.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level `2`, which equates to a standard baseline radius of `0.5rem` (8px), striking a clean equilibrium between crisp architectural bookselling forms and friendly accessibility.

### Shape Scale Rules
- **Base Elements (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Applied to buttons, search fields, card containers, dialog boxes, and image thumbnails.
- **Large Containers (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Curated hero banners, reading sample overlays, and promotional feature blocks.
- **Sheet Drawers (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Top corners of mobile bottom sheets and slide-out discovery filters.
- **Pills (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for genre chips, status tags, format badges (Hardcover, Audio, Paperback), and pagination indicators.
- **Book Covers (Special Case):** Bound book covers strictly feature `2px` to `4px` subtle corner rounding to retain realistic print fidelity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid terracotta (`#C05A3E`) background, pure white (`#FFFFFF`) text, `rounded-md` (8px), medium height (44px), font weight 600. On hover, background shifts to `#AA4B31` with a soft lift. Focus outline is 2px offset terracotta.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline):** Transparent background with `1.5px solid #2C2A29`, text `#2C2A29`. On hover, background transitions to `#F2EFEB` with border `#2C2A29`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#C05A3E` or `#2B4C3F`. Hover reveals an ultra-faint wash of `#C05A3E` at 8% opacity.

### Chips & Badges
- **Genre & Filter Chips:** Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`), border `1px solid #E6E0D8`, text `#2C2A29`, fully rounded pill (`9999px`), padding `6px 14px`. Active state shifts to solid `#2B4C3F` or `#C05A3E` with white text.
- **Format Badges (Hardcover / eBook / Audiobook):** Compact micro-pills with `label-sm` font, soft tinted background (`#F2EFEB`), text `#2C2A29`, borderless.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search Bar:** Height 48px, pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`), hairline border `1px solid #E6E0D8`, rounded `8px`, text `#2C2A29`, placeholder text `#8C8782`. Focus state presents a crisp `1px solid #C05A3E` border paired with an ambient 3px warm ring (`rgba(192, 90, 62, 0.15)`).
- **Form Controls:** Labels positioned above inputs in `title-md` or `label-md`. Assistive helper text set in `body-sm`. Error states display deep rust red border `#B33A3A` with red helper text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px squares with `4px` radius, border `1.5px solid #B0A9A0`. Checked state renders solid `#C05A3E` with a pure white check icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** 18x18px circles with a 6px central solid dot in `#C05A3E` when selected.

### Product Cards (Book Showcase)
- **Structure:** Surface white (`#FFFFFF`), border `1px solid #E6E0D8`, padding `16px`, `rounded-md` (8px).
- **Cover Display:** Top visual zone featuring the cover with aspect ratio `1:1.48`, resting on an ultra-light recessed ivory plinth (`#F8F6F2`).
- **Typography:** Book title in `Libre Caslon Text` (`title-md` or `headline-sm`), author name in `Manrope` (`body-sm` neutral muted), and pricing displayed in bold `Manrope` (`title-md`) alongside format availability.
- **Interactive Action:** Quick "Add to Cart" or "Save to Shelf" actions reveal seamlessly on hover on desktop or remain accessible via a subtle bookmark icon.

### Reading Quotes & Editorial Banners
- Dedicated editorial callout blocks incorporating left-border embellishments in deep forest green (`#2B4C3F`, 3px wide) over `#F2EFEB` background with pull-quotes set in italic `Libre Caslon Text`.
